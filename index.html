Underwriting Methodology Note

1. Conservative ARV: Derived from live submarket $/SqFt medians with a 0.92x (8%) conservative haircut so distressed properties are never over-valued.

2. Standard 70% ARV Rule: MAO = (ARV × 0.70) − Rehab Cost. Gives flippers and wholesalers a built-in 30% margin buffer for profit, financing, and closing friction.

3. Net Flip Profit & ROI: Net Profit = ARV − Purchase Price − Rehab Cost − Closing/Holding Costs (default 9% combined buy/sell/holding allowance; fully editable on the right).
